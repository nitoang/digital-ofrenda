What this is

Keepers and successors

The owner of an ofrenda can email its link to people to view it, and can invite co-keepers, who can change it, and can name one successor. Anyone who has an ofrenda’s link can view it, whether or not the owner sent it to them. A successor has no access unless they agree. Once they agree they become a co-keeper, who can change the ofrenda but not delete it, and the ofrenda can only pass to them after the owner has been away for a year (or six months, if the owner chose that) and has been told, with 30 days to stop it by signing in. Deleting an account does not erase an ofrenda that other people keep: it is passed on to them. An owner can also hand an ofrenda to someone, who becomes an owner beside the giver. If that person has no account and does not sign up within 90 days, the ofrenda goes to the signed-in person who opened it on the most days, or if nobody did, to the successor, then to the co-keepers, and otherwise stays with the giver; the owner has to type a word to confirm a handover. Owners are responsible for who they invite. An ofrenda that nobody else keeps is deleted with its owner’s account, and the site warns you before it does.
